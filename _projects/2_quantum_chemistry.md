Classical force fields are fast but systematically wrong in places that matter —
polarization, charge transfer, unusual chemistries. Quantum mechanics gets those
right, and the interesting engineering question is where to spend it.

I integrate QM methods with **antibody sequence optimization**, capturing the
fine-grained electronic and structural properties that drive binding affinity,
stability, and specificity.

Earlier, at the Flatiron Institute, I applied the same idea to **cyclic peptides**:
building Rosetta applications that design crosslinkers by tuning the Rosetta force
field against quantum-chemical reference data, supporting potency and stability
optimization in therapeutic programs.
