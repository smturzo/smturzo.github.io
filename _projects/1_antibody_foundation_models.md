Antibody discovery is a search problem over an enormous sequence space, and the
foundation-model era has changed what is tractable. My work here has two halves.

**Benchmarking.** Before trusting a model, you have to know where it breaks. I design
and run rigorous benchmarking studies of state-of-the-art foundation models for
antibody design, evaluating whether they can genuinely accelerate development of
bispecific, trispecific, and other complex antibody therapeutics.

**Building.** I lead development of a novel foundation model architecture built
specifically for antibody discovery — optimizing model performance and establishing
the scaling laws that make training efficient rather than merely expensive.
